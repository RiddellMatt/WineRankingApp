Decanti

A cellar tracker for bottles you have tasted

Decanti is not a warehouse inventory. It is the record of what you drank: the ranking, the place, and whether you would order it again.

Regions collect into a wine passport. Friends can see the bottles you choose to share, and you can see when someone you know pulls ahead on a country or a style. Your notes stay private until you share a wine.

The free cellar holds 20 wines. That is enough to learn the shape of your taste. Pro removes the cap and adds label scans and insights.
