What to order from the wine list

A restaurant wine list is a hard place to decide. The names are unfamiliar, the table is waiting, and the one bottle you remember liking is not on the page.

Decanti reads a photo of the list and lines those bottles up with the wines you have already rated. The top pick comes with a short reason, based on your cellar rather than a generic score. Two of those scans are free. Decanti Pro includes more each month.

Share the pick with the table. The card links back here, and a friend who rates a wine with your invite gets a month of Pro with you.
