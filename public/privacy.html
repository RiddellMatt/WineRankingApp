Decanti Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

Decanti (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) is a wine journal and ranking app published under the bundle identifier com.northline.decanti. This policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use Decanti on the web, iOS, or Android.

Information we collect

  • Account data. When you sign in (for example with Google or Apple), we receive identifiers and profile basics needed to create and secure your account. Account data is stored using our backend provider (Supabase).
  • Wine journal data. Ratings, notes, cellar entries, wish lists, and related content you choose to save in the app.
  • Photos you provide. If you scan labels or menus, images may be processed to extract wine information. You control when the camera or photo library is used.
  • Push notification tokens. If you enable notifications, we store a device token so we can deliver alerts you opt into.
  • Subscription status. If you purchase Decanti Pro, payment is processed by Apple (App Store), Google (Play), or Stripe on web. We receive information needed to confirm your subscription tier, not your full payment card number.

Advertising (free accounts)

Free accounts may see banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers and usage data to deliver and measure ads, subject to your consent choices where required (for example in the European Economic Area). Decanti Pro subscribers are not shown in-app banner ads when the app is configured for ad-free Pro.

You can learn more about how Google uses data in ads at Google’s partner sites policy.

How we use information

  • Provide and sync your cellar, rankings, and profile across devices.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Send optional push notifications you enable.
  • Process subscriptions and restore purchases.
  • Show ads to free users and comply with consent requirements.
  • Improve reliability and fix errors.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run Decanti, such as:

  • Supabase (hosting and authentication)
  • Google and Apple (sign-in)
  • Google AdMob (advertising for free users)
  • RevenueCat, Apple, Google, and/or Stripe (subscriptions, depending on platform)
  • Apple Push Notification service / Firebase Cloud Messaging (push delivery)

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • Consent for ads. In regions that require it, you may see a consent form before personalized ads are requested. Your choices are stored by Google’s consent tools.
  • Apple tracking. On iOS, you may be asked whether to allow tracking across apps; you can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Account deletion. Contact us (below) to request deletion of your account and associated journal data, subject to legal retention needs.

Children

Decanti is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We use industry-standard practices through our providers to protect data in transit and at rest. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version at this URL and update the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the developer using the support email or URL listed on Decanti’s App Store or Google Play listing, or open an issue on our public repository at github.com/RiddellMatt/WineRankingApp.
