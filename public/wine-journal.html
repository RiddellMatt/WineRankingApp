Decanti

A wine journal that stays ranked

A wine journal is only useful if you can find the bottle later. Decanti keeps the winery, vintage, region, price, and what you thought, then ranks the list so the wines you would buy again sit at the top.

Ratings run from 1 to 10. Notes stay on the bottle. A want-to-try list holds the wines you have not opened yet, so a shop trip starts from your own taste instead of the shelf talker.

The same account works on iPhone, Android, and the web. Rate one bottle here, then add Decanti to your home screen or open it in the app when the store listing is available.
