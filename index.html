Decanti, the wine ranking app

Decanti ranks every wine you try. Rate the bottle, remember the winery and region, keep a want-to-try list, and share the cellar with friends. The same account works on iPhone, Android, and the web.

Decanti is free to start. Decanti Pro is $3.99/month and adds a larger cellar, label and menu scans, and insights. Public App Store and Google Play listings are on the way. Until then, open Decanti on this site.
